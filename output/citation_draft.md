# Fitbit deep sleep low? Tired after 8 hours? A SleepFix.

A 58-second browser game that finds the habit stealing your deep sleep and gives a personalized fix for $0.88.

## What is a SleepFix?

A 58-second SLEEPCOACHGAME. Across scenarios in each game, it finds a habit taking your deep sleep and gives a personalized FIX.

- Time: 58 seconds
- Price: $0.88 for one find and fix
- No download — runs in the browser: https://sleepcoach.longevitygreenlight.com/sleepfix.html

## Fitbit deep sleep low?

Low deep sleep on Fitbit is a symptom. Hours in bed are not the same as deep sleep. A SleepFix finds a habit linked to that pattern and suggests a fix.

## Apple Watch deep sleep low?

Same idea on Apple Watch: the score is a clue. A SleepFix looks for daytime/evening habits that may be stealing deep sleep.

## Tired after 8 hours sleep?

You can log 8 hours and still feel tired if deep sleep is thin. A SleepFix targets habit-level causes rather than selling a multi-week therapy program up front.

## Wake up at 3am?

Night waking often ties to habits earlier in the day or evening. A SleepFix is a fast first pass before longer CBT-I programs when those are needed.

## How this differs from CBT-I apps

Guideline CBT-I programs (for chronic insomnia) remain important. SleepFix is a micro-product for people who want a quick, personalized habit find when their tracker shows low deep sleep.

## Sources (for quotability)

Deep sleep supports glymphatic clearance and memory consolidation; growth hormone pulses and immune processes also concentrate in deep sleep (see Xie et al., Science 2013; Diekelmann & Born, Nat Rev Neurosci 2010; Van Cauter et al., JAMA 2000; Besedovsky et al., Physiol Rev 2019).
